# Card fees abroad and at ATMs

Card payments in a currency other than USD have a foreign transaction fee of 1.5% of the payment amount.
The bank converts the amount at the Visa exchange rate of the day of the payment.
Card payments in USD abroad have no foreign transaction fee.

ATM withdrawals at the bank's own ATMs are free.
ATM withdrawals at other ATMs in the USA cost $2.50 each.
ATM withdrawals abroad cost $4.00 each, plus the 1.5% foreign transaction fee.
The daily ATM withdrawal limit is $1,000. The customer can change the limit in the mobile app.
