# Replacement of a lost or stolen card

When a customer reports a lost or stolen card, the bank blocks the card immediately and orders a new card.
The first replacement card in a calendar year is free. Each further replacement in the same year costs $10.

Standard delivery takes 5 to 7 business days.
Express delivery takes 2 business days and costs $25.
The new card has a new card number. The PIN does not change.
A virtual card is available in the mobile app at once, so the customer can pay online before the new card arrives.
