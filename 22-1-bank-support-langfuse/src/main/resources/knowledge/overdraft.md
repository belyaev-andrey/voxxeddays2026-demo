# Overdraft

Customers with a regular monthly income can use an overdraft of up to $500.
The overdraft interest rate is 18.9% per year, calculated daily on the negative balance.
The bank charges no fixed overdraft fee.

The bank sends a push notification when the balance goes below $0.
The bank sends a second notification when the customer uses 80% of the overdraft limit.
The customer can switch off the overdraft in the mobile app at any time when the balance is positive.
