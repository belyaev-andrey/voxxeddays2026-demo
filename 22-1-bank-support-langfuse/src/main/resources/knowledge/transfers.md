# Money transfers

Domestic ACH transfers are free and arrive in 1 to 2 business days.
Domestic wire transfers cost $15 and arrive on the same business day when sent before 4:00 PM Eastern Time.

SEPA transfers in EUR to a European bank cost $5 and arrive in 1 business day.
SWIFT transfers to other countries cost $35 and arrive in 2 to 5 business days.
The receiving bank can charge an additional fee for SWIFT transfers.

The cut-off time for international transfers is 2:00 PM Eastern Time.
The bank processes transfers sent after the cut-off time on the next business day.
